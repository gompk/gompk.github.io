    How to play

    AvID (Aves Identifier) uses citizen science observations of birds around the world to test your field knowledge skills.

    Look at the image, and listen to the bird calls, then guess the species!

    You can use common names or scientific nomenclature. It's okay if you don't know — try to get as close as possible on the taxonomic tree. You can use the pick me button to pick the right bird by matching it to a picture.

    You can practice in your own backyard by narrowing the region, or focus on a specific branch of the tree by selecting the mode.

    Increasing the rarity will increase your chances of encountering rare birds.
